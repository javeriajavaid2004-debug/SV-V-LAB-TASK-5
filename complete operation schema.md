# Task 2: Complete Operation Schema

| Op-ID | Operation                           | Purpose                                 | Precondition                                                       | Event/Input                                             | Post-Condition                                                    |
| ----- | ----------------------------------- | --------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------ | ------------------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------- |
| OP-01 | Perform Sensor Self-Check           | Check essential sensors.                | Chamber is powered on.                                             | Power-on event; sensor responses.                       | All essential sensors are verified or a fault is identified.      |
| OP-02 | Check Environmental-Control Devices | Verify control devices.                 | Chamber is powered on.                                             | Device status and test response.                        | Required control devices are verified.                            |
| OP-03 | Register Artifact                   | Record artifact information.            | Artifact is placed inside chamber.                                 | Artifact ID and identification information.             | Artifact is registered.                                           |
| OP-04 | Load Environmental Profile          | Load required environmental limits.     | Artifact is registered.                                            | Temperature and humidity limits.                        | Environmental profile is available for monitoring.                |
| OP-05 | Check Chamber Door Status           | Determine door condition.               | Door sensor is working.                                            | Door sensor reading.                                    | Current door status is known.                                     |
| OP-06 | Monitor Temperature                 | Monitor chamber temperature.            | Temperature sensor and profile are available.                      | Current temperature reading.                            | Temperature status is recorded.                                   |
| OP-07 | Monitor Humidity                    | Monitor chamber humidity.               | Humidity sensor and profile are available.                         | Current humidity reading.                               | Humidity status is recorded.                                      |
| OP-08 | Monitor Light Exposure              | Monitor light affecting artifact.       | Light sensor is working; artifact is inside.                       | Current light reading.                                  | Light exposure status is recorded.                                |
| OP-09 | Monitor Vibration                   | Monitor chamber vibration.              | Vibration sensor is working; artifact is inside.                   | Current vibration reading.                              | Vibration status is recorded.                                     |
| OP-10 | Monitor Artifact Condition          | Monitor artifact condition.             | Artifact is registered; condition sensor is working.               | Artifact condition reading.                             | Current artifact condition is recorded.                           |
| OP-11 | Detect Temperature Violation        | Identify temperature outside limits.    | Temperature monitoring is active.                                  | Temperature reading outside permitted range.            | Temperature violation is detected and correction can begin.       |
| OP-12 | Correct Temperature                 | Restore required temperature.           | Temperature is outside permitted range; control is available.      | Temperature violation.                                  | Temperature returns to range or recovery failure is identified.   |
| OP-13 | Verify Temperature Recovery         | Confirm temperature recovery.           | Temperature correction has been attempted.                         | New temperature reading and recovery time.              | Temperature recovery is confirmed or failed.                      |
| OP-14 | Detect Humidity Violation           | Identify humidity outside limits.       | Humidity monitoring is active.                                     | Humidity reading outside permitted range.               | Humidity violation is detected and correction can begin.          |
| OP-15 | Correct Humidity                    | Restore required humidity.              | Humidity is outside permitted range; control is available.         | Humidity violation.                                     | Humidity returns to range or recovery failure is identified.      |
| OP-16 | Verify Humidity Recovery            | Confirm humidity recovery.              | Humidity correction has been attempted.                            | New humidity reading and recovery time.                 | Humidity recovery is confirmed or failed.                         |
| OP-17 | Activate Protection Controls        | Protect artifact after failed recovery. | Environmental condition cannot be corrected within allowed period. | Recovery failure.                                       | Additional protection controls are active.                        |
| OP-18 | Generate Operator Alert             | Inform museum operator.                 | Critical condition is detected.                                    | Protection event or critical incident.                  | Alert is generated and recorded.                                  |
| OP-19 | Detect Significant Vibration        | Detect dangerous vibration.             | Artifact is inside; vibration sensor is working.                   | Vibration exceeds permitted threshold.                  | Significant vibration is detected.                                |
| OP-20 | Suspend Conservation Activities     | Stop risky conservation activities.     | Door is open or significant vibration is detected.                 | Door-open event or vibration event.                     | Normal conservation activities are suspended.                     |
| OP-21 | Verify Vibration Stabilization      | Confirm stable vibration.               | Vibration response is active.                                      | Vibration readings below threshold for required period. | Stabilization is confirmed or monitoring continues.               |
| OP-22 | Verify Environmental Conditions     | Check safe environmental conditions.    | Chamber door is closed again or response condition has ended.      | Temperature, humidity, and light readings.              | Environmental safety is confirmed or rejected.                    |
| OP-23 | Verify Sensor Status                | Confirm sensors are ready.              | System needs to resume normal operation.                           | Sensor status/readings.                                 | Sensor readiness is confirmed or fault is identified.             |
| OP-24 | Switch to Emergency Power           | Maintain operation after power loss.    | Main power is unavailable; emergency power is available.           | Main power failure event.                               | System operates using emergency power.                            |
| OP-25 | Record Power Failure                | Record power failure incident.          | Main power has failed.                                             | Power failure event.                                    | Power failure information is stored.                              |
| OP-26 | Perform Safe Shutdown               | Safely stop system during power loss.   | Main and emergency power are unavailable.                          | Power failure with no emergency power.                  | Normal conservation stops safely and incident is recorded.        |
| OP-27 | Verify Safe Artifact Removal        | Confirm artifact can be removed safely. | Operator requests removal.                                         | Removal request; chamber and safety status.             | Removal is allowed only when all safety conditions are satisfied. |
